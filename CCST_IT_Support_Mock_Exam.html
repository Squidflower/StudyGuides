A realistic, scenario-based dry run across all six exam domains. Questions and answer choices are drawn fresh and shuffled each attempt, so you can retake it without memorizing positions.

How it works

  • Questions are pulled at random from a larger pool and mixed across all 6 domains.
  • A countdown timer mirrors the real 50-minute limit; when it hits zero the exam auto-submits.
  • Answers are not graded until you submit — you can change them and flag questions to revisit.
  • Pass mark here is set to 70% as a study benchmark (Cisco reports the real exam as pass/fail only).
  • Many items are scenario-based — pick what a good technician would actually do (escalate, document, get consent, protect PII).
  • After submitting you get a score, a per-domain breakdown, and a full answer review with explanations.
